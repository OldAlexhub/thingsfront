Make room for a day less ordinary.

Things to Do: Day Planner helps you discover experiences and put together a day around your interests, time, group and admission budget. Start with Orlando, Florida, and explore new destinations as they become available.

DISCOVER YOUR KIND OF DAY
Browse server-published attractions, outdoor places, food, upcoming events, hotels and curated collections. Search by category and filter activities by known price, duration, setting, age suitability and location. Check hotel rates directly with the property. Available content and event calendars vary by destination.

PLAN WITH A LITTLE MORE THOUGHT
Choose your date, starting point, group, pace and interests. Build an itinerary from listed experiences, with admission estimates, breaks and clearly labeled rough travel estimates. Replace, remove or reorder stops and recalculate your day. Open the ordered stops in Google Maps; longer days open in numbered parts.

KEEP THE GOOD IDEAS
Save favorites, build a bucket list and organize custom collections. Mark places as visited and keep a personal date, name and note. Create a photo memory card that says you were here, with #old_alex_hub, then share it through your chosen app or save the image. Save day plans to read offline, share them or export a file. No public account is required.

KNOW BEFORE YOU GO
Prices, hours, events and availability can change. Unknown details are identified. Confirm plans with venues and ticket providers before traveling. Itineraries are suggestions, not reservations, and admission estimates exclude meals, travel, taxes and fees.

Free with advertising when enabled. Internet access is required for fresh discovery, planning and maps. Personal saved content stays on your device; the app also communicates with its backend, map providers and advertising services.
